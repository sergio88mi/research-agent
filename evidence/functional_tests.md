# Functional test log

Live, end-to-end tests of the built system, run from the terminal against the
real services (OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref, Gemini). Unit tests
(`pytest`, 113 tests, recorded API responses, no network) cover the same
behaviours in isolation; these tests confirm them in the running system.
Observed results are copied from the terminal / `output/run.log`; the
screenshots and log excerpts that exist are in this folder. Results that were
only shown on the screen (the stop messages in FT-01 and FT-08, the re-shown
list in FT-02, the refused second `reject` in FT-04) were copied by hand at
the time and have no capture; the log lines around them are in `output/run.log`. FT-01 to FT-09 were run on the
single-source system (Stages 1-6); FT-10 and FT-11 on the two-source system
(Stage 7); all of those used the one research question shown in FT-05 (FT-01
used the word "test"). FT-12 to FT-16 were run on 2 Oct 2026 on the submitted
code, after the read-through of 1 Oct, with
`bash evidence/live_tests_20261002.sh`: the ordinary command with the answers
to the two reviews piped in, so the commands can be repeated exactly (the
results would differ: the replies are now in the cache and the services change). What the
terminal showed is in `ft12_...log` to `ft16_...log` in this folder. FT-13 to
FT-15 use three other research questions.

## Summary

Sixteen tests, all PASS (FT-01 to FT-09 between 17 and 23 Sept 2026, dates in each row; FT-10 and FT-11 on 30 Sept 2026 after Stage 7; FT-12 to FT-16 on 2 Oct 2026 on the submitted code). Two carry a qualification. FT-03 shows the revision loop working, not that feedback pointing at sub-question numbers was followed (remediation #18); FT-12 repeats it with the fix. FT-14 passes for the option itself and shows that a constraint steers the search without filtering what comes back. Along the way the live tests produced remediations #8-#11 (model quota, OpenAlex key and retry policy, Review 1 typo handling) - see `remediation_log.md`. The screening of FT-13 was afterwards checked by a second model; see the last section.

## Notes

- Every Gemini request counts against the free daily quota, retries included. On 22 Sept the Stage 6 run (15 requests: 5 answered, 10 refused with a 503) plus the FT-01 mis-run (7) exhausted it, so the remaining live tests are spread over separate days or run on a model with a larger free allowance (`GEMINI_MODEL` in `.env`, no code change).
- Cached results (plan, searches, verification, scores for the standard question) make re-runs cheap: only new prompts cost requests.
- Semantic Scholar without a key shares one public rate pool; in FT-10 it refused 3 of 5 searches with 429 even at one request every few seconds. A free key (`S2_API_KEY`, 1 request/second dedicated) is the fix; failover means the run completes either way, with narrower coverage recorded in the brief.
- FT-01 to FT-11 were not repeated after the read-through of 1 Oct (remediations #12-#21). FT-12 to FT-16 ran on the corrected code on 2 Oct, and the offline replay below checks that the corrections left the 30 Sept result unchanged. Seen live on 2 Oct: #12 (FT-16), the single-rewrite cap of #16 (FT-14: sub-question 2 stayed below the threshold and was not rewritten a second time), #17 (FT-14), #18 at Review 1 (FT-12), and from #21 the reworded limitation sentence, the constraint in the brief and the end of input (FT-12, FT-14, FT-16). FT-12 also met four replies stored before #13 and repaired them again; that the validated reply is now the one kept would only show in a second run, and there was none. Covered by unit tests only: #14 and #15 (their cases did not arise: no title in another script, no live answer from Semantic Scholar, no scope revision), the error text of #19 (no scholarly API returned a non-transient error), the interrupted cache write of #20, Ctrl-C in the middle of a run, and a revision after a scope rejection at Review 2. Three of the older tests would look different on screen today: a run that stops ends with one line, `Run stopped: <message>`, instead of a traceback (FT-01, FT-08; shown in FT-16), and the 404 line of FT-11 no longer shows the query string (#19).
- 2 Oct, FT-12 to FT-15: Semantic Scholar's public pool refused every live search, 16 of 16 (the one search it answered, in FT-12, came from the cache). Failover worked each time, at about 49 s per search (45 s of waits plus the requests), so those four briefs rest on OpenAlex alone for the new searches and say so. Without a key the second source added nothing new that day: the 19 Semantic Scholar papers approved in FT-12 come from the cached answer of 30 Sept.
- 2 Oct: the four runs made 74 model requests. 18 of the 56 first replies came back as a bare list instead of an object and were repaired on the single retry; none needed a second.
- DOIs verified in Crossref, by question (2 Oct): 46 of 74 for the hallucination question (20 of the 21 unverified approved papers are arXiv DOIs), 65 of 65 for telemedicine, 101 of 104 for food waste, 75 of 77 for remote work.
